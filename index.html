About

Raw data in. Trusted data out.

I’m a data engineer with 5+ years across multi-source integration and Salesforce data migration. I design Medallion pipelines on Azure Databricks with Delta Lake, tune Spark until it’s fast, and add the data-quality checks that keep it right.

On the CRM side I plan and run large Salesforce migrations end to end: source-to-target mapping, cleansing, deduplication, loads and full reconciliation, through SIT, UAT and production.

A migration isn’t done until every record is accounted for.

How I build

Sources to gold, then into Salesforce.

The Medallion pattern I use: land raw data, clean and conform it, then publish analytics-ready and migration-ready tables. Hover a stage.
